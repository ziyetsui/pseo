third-party material. The CMS rights decision records the applicable path,
reviewer, UTC time, evidence, policy version and exact content/source revision.

`community_attributed` Prompt text, third-party media, trademarks, likenesses or
quotations are covered by CC BY 4.0. Each such generated entry must display its
author, original source, “author retains rights” notice and takedown route.

This policy is not legal advice. Material with unclear rights remains
`review_required`; restricted or takedown material is excluded from the public
snapshot.
